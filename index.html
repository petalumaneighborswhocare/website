How it works

Five steps, four times a year. No committees, no galas, no overhead.

  1. Join

    Pledge $100 each quarter through Grapevine.

  2. Nominate

    Members suggest local nonprofits that serve Petaluma and southern Sonoma County.

  3. Three are chosen

    About two weeks before each meeting, three nominees are picked to present.

  4. Hear them

    At the meeting, each nominee shares how they would use the gift.

  5. Vote and give

    Members vote. The whole pot goes out the door.

One vote. Three gifts.

The top vote getter receives most of the pot, and the two runners up each get a meaningful share, so every presenter leaves with something.

Nominate a local nonprofit

Any member can nominate a group that serves Petaluma or southern Sonoma County. Tell us who they are, what they do, and why they matter to our neighbors.

A good nominee

A registered 501(c)(3), or a group with a fiscal sponsor. Serves Petaluma or southern Sonoma County. Has a clear plan for how a gift of several thousand dollars would help people here.

To nominate

Email petalumaneighborswhocare@gmail.com with the group's name, website, and a sentence or two about why you are nominating them. About two weeks before each meeting, three nominees are chosen from the list to present.

Meetings

We meet four times a year. Each meeting runs about an hour: time to meet your neighbors, three short nonprofit presentations, questions, a vote, and a sendoff. Members and curious neighbors thinking about joining are welcome. Bring a friend.

Questions

Who can join?

Anyone who lives or works within about 20 minutes of Petaluma and wants to give back locally. Everyone is welcome.

How much does it cost?

Each member gives $100 per meeting, four meetings a year, which is $400 a year.

Where does the money go?

All of it goes to local nonprofits. The top vote getter receives 70 percent of the pot, and each of the two runners up receives 15 percent.

How do I pay?

Joining and giving happen through Grapevine, a secure giving platform. Use the Join button on this page.

Is my gift tax deductible?

Payments go through Grapevine, which processes them through the Grapevine Giving Foundation, a 501(c)(3) public charity, and you receive a receipt by email. Ask your tax professional how that applies to your own return.

Do I have to go to every meeting?

No. Come when you can. Your gift still counts.

Which nonprofits can be nominated?

Groups that serve Petaluma and southern Sonoma County.

How are the three finalists chosen?

About two weeks before each meeting, three nominees are chosen from the submitted nominations.
