You nominate. Chance picks. You vote.

Members nominate nonprofits. About two weeks before each meeting, three are drawn at random from the nominations. Every nomination is one entry, so what you think shapes the draw: a nonprofit that many members nominate has better odds. At the meeting, members hear all three and vote.

The split

The top vote getter receives 70% of the pot. The two runners up each receive 15%.
