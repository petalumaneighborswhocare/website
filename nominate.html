Nominate

Nominate a local nonprofit.

Every quarter, three nonprofits present to our members and the whole pot goes to local work. The nominee could be one you volunteer with, one that helped your family, or one you wish more people knew about.

Click the button to open a short email with the questions filled in. Answer what you can. We take it from there.

What makes a good nominee

The group

  • A registered 501(c)(3), or a group with a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor.
  • Serves people in Petaluma or southern Sonoma County.
  • Has a clear plan for how a gift of several thousand dollars would help people here.
  • Can send someone to present for a few minutes at the meeting.

How it works

  1. Anyone can nominate. Tell us who they are and why they matter.
  2. About two weeks before the meeting, three nominees are drawn at random. Each nomination is one entry, so more nominations means better odds.
  3. At the meeting, members hear each one and vote.
  4. The top vote getter receives 70% of the pot. The two runners up each receive 15%.
